# The Good Ship — case studies and testimonials to collect

**Working document for outreach and editorial approval. Not public website content.**

The new service pages are deliberately published **without** testimonials or invented client outcomes. Evidence can be added as it becomes available and approved.

## Two useful kinds of proof

**A case study** explains a real problem, what was tried, how decisions were made, what changed and what remains uncertain. It works especially well when it shows the quality of the practice rather than claiming a dramatic percentage improvement.

**A testimonial** is a short, authentic quotation from someone who worked with The Good Ship. It adds their perspective. Do not write a quote on someone else's behalf without asking them to approve the exact wording.

A useful first set would cover distinct areas:

- Responsible AI / technology choices / team capability.
- Data strategy, architecture, data-sharing or practical mapping.
- Organisational resilience, facilitation, collective learning or strategic decisions.
- A public product or Shipyard build as an additional technical example.

Keep examples anonymous if needed, but distinguish anonymised real work from openly built projects. Never imply an individual engagement is a repeatable product outcome.

## Case study interview / collection template

**Draft title:** A specific problem and approach, not a superlative.

**Organisation:** Name, type and approximate size, where publication is permitted.

**Context:** Who was involved? What was happening? Why did the work matter?

**Initial challenge:** What was not working, and what made it difficult to solve?

**Constraints:** Time, capacity, governance, budget, technology, data sensitivity, politics or uncertainty.

**What we did:** What was the Good Ship's actual contribution? Separate facilitation/advice/design/build activities from what the organisation did itself.

**Decisions and trade-offs:** What alternatives were considered? Why was this approach selected? What was explicitly ruled out?

**What changed:** Observable outcomes and evidence. If numbers are available, identify the source, period and permission to publish. If not, explain practical qualitative changes.

**What did not change / what was learned:** A credible story can include an unresolved problem or a decision not to build.

**Useful public links:** Consent-approved tools, publications, anonymised diagrams or frameworks relevant to the story.

**Quote:** Exact wording supplied or approved by the contributor.

**Follow-up:** What did the organisation do afterwards, and what is still evolving?

**Status:** Draft / fact-check / approval pending / approved to publish.

## Four questions for a testimonial

1. What problem or decision led us to work together?
2. What was different about the way I approached the work?
3. What became easier, clearer or possible as a result?
4. Is there anything you would say to another organisation considering similar support?

Ask for a quotation in their own words. A few specific, human sentences are more persuasive than vague praise.

## Publication consent — confirm explicitly

- Organisation name may / may not be published.
- Contributor's name, role and organisation may / may not be published.
- Exact quotation approved for public website, including attribution.
- Details of the work and any figures verified for publication.
- Links and screenshots approved, with privacy and intellectual property checked.
- Permission for use on the relevant Good Ship service page and, if agreed, elsewhere on the Good Ship site.
- Contact for corrections and any future withdrawal request.
- Date and source of approval recorded privately, not in public JSON.

A publicly named logo is not the same as permission to publish a case study or a quotation.

## How we will place the approved work

| Service page | Useful proof | What to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/ai-technology-strategy/` | A policy, a decision, an adoption process or evidence of staff capability | Unsupported claims that AI saved a fixed amount of time |
| `/data-strategy/` | Improved information flow, common definition, reusable dataset or clearer reporting | Made-up before-and-after numbers |
| `/organisational-resilience/` | A difficult decision, shift in priorities, strengthened coordination or a repeatable learning process | Claims of guaranteed organisational resilience |
| `/shipyard/` | An actual build, ownership/handover approach, adoption and maintenance lessons | Passing off an open experiment as a client delivery |

Each published story should have its own descriptive HTML page (if substantial), a unique canonical URL, explicit author/attribution and a sensible link to the relevant service. We can add structured `Article` or `CreativeWork` data when the story exists; we should not pre-populate review/ratings schema.

## Fast first pass

Send one example per service, even as rough notes, and any quotes that contributors have approved. Include the scope of what can be named publicly. We can then turn those into clear, accurate site content and ask the contributor to confirm the final copy.
