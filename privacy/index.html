Who we are

The Good Ship Ltd (company number 12415213), registered in England and Wales, is responsible for personal information collected through good-ship.co.uk, our own consultancy communications and the tools we operate where we determine how and why information is used. Our registered office is 19 Bickford Terrace, Aycliffe, Newton Aycliffe, England, DL5 6LA. You can contact us about privacy at tom@good-ship.co.uk.

Different tools, different data. This is our general company and website notice. Website technologies and recipients

Our main site is served using Cloudflare. Newsletter subscription records are stored in Cloudflare D1. The website loads Plausible Analytics, which is designed for aggregate measurement without analytics cookies. Some pages load Google Fonts. If you sponsor work or follow a booking link, the relevant forms and payment service will also process the information required to provide that service, under its own privacy terms.

Other service providers may include our email, hosting, database, AI or technical support services where needed for a specific engagement or tool. We limit their access to the purpose for which they are used, and product notices give further detail when appropriate.

How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed for its purpose and any legal record-keeping requirements. Mailing-list addresses remain until you unsubscribe or ask for removal; contact correspondence is reviewed for continued usefulness; accounting records are retained for the period required by applicable tax and company law. Different product-specific retention arrangements are explained in their notices. We do not promise a fixed deletion timetable unless we can actually implement it.

International processing

Some infrastructure and service providers may process information outside the UK. Where UK data-protection law requires a transfer mechanism, we assess the relevant provider arrangements and use applicable safeguards. You can ask us for information about a particular transfer.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances and the lawful basis, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of personal information. You can withdraw consent at any time for processing based on consent. You have a right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Please email tom@good-ship.co.uk; we may need to verify your identity before responding.

You can complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). We would welcome the chance to address concerns directly first.

Changes and individual services

We update this notice when our practices change and show a revision date. If you use a separate Good Ship product, please read the product-specific notice as well. Nothing in this general notice grants permission to transfer personal information from one product into another.
