Event privacy notice

How the Event race-preparation app uses training, event and recovery information, with clear limits for planned Garmin access and AI assistance.

Last reviewed: 30 October 2026 · Applies as explained below

In brief. Event is a race-preparation and training-planning app operated by The Good Ship Ltd. You choose whether to connect an activity provider. Event compares the demands of your chosen event with your training evidence to propose preparation priorities. Direct Garmin Connect data access is planned but not currently active; the sections about Garmin describe the proposed integration, not a claim that it is already collecting data.

Who controls your data

The Good Ship Ltd, registered in England and Wales (company number 12415213), is responsible for Event's personal-data processing. Our registered office is 19 Bickford Terrace, Aycliffe, Newton Aycliffe, England, DL5 6LA. Privacy enquiries: tom@good-ship.co.uk. This notice supplements our general privacy notice.

Information Event uses

  • Account and settings: sign-in identifier, email and profile details, your race selections, scheduling preferences and saved workspaces.
  • Race preparation: event date, distance, course/elevation evidence, goals, training plans, completed sessions, personal notes and fuelling trials you choose to enter.
  • Connected training activity: with your authorisation, activity summaries from Strava, such as dates, duration, distance, ascent, pace and available heart-rate readings. Selected activity streams can help assess climbing and pacing.
  • Health and recovery: optional subjective check-ins and physiological or recovery signals, where available, requested and permitted. Some of these may qualify as special-category health data.
  • Technical records: account sessions, provider connection tokens and limited operational or security records necessary to protect accounts and deliver the service.

Event currently analyses imported GPX in the browser and retains derived relative geometry and elevation rather than storing the original GPX track coordinates in its saved workspace. Sensitive details should not be included in notes unless they are needed for the chosen feature.

Why we use it and legal bases

We use account and training information to provide the Event features you request, keep your saved training plan, display evidence and reconcile completed sessions (normally performance of a contract or pre-contract steps, Article 6(1)(b)). We use limited technical and security data to safeguard the service (legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f)). Optional communications and separate uses of information are based on consent where appropriate.

Special-category health information needs an additional Article 9 condition. Where an optional import or analysis processes special-category health data, our intended condition is your explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a), alongside an Article 6 lawful basis. The Garmin Health integration must not be enabled until separate, recorded consent and withdrawal controls have been implemented and verified. Connecting an account alone should not be treated as blanket permission for unrelated processing.

Strava and the planned Garmin connection

Event's current Strava integration uses OAuth authorisation to obtain the activity information you choose to share for training analysis. Event does not ask for your Strava password. Disconnecting the provider stops future retrieval by Event, subject to the provider's own revocation process; previously imported data and derived training evidence may still need a separate deletion request.

For future Garmin support, we intend to request the Activity API (completed runs and training evidence), Training API (send your approved structured workouts to Garmin Connect) and Health API (optional recovery indicators, only after additional explicit consent). The Courses API may be used later for athlete-approved course delivery. We are not requesting Women's Health data for Event's initial use case. Garmin, as the source platform, operates under its own separate privacy policy.

Once approved and implemented, Garmin connection must use Garmin's authorised OAuth flow, restrict received data to the chosen features, respect withdrawal, and provide clear deletion and access options. The Starter programme does not provide historical import, so it must not be presented as if it does.

AI transparency — especially important for Garmin data

AI assistance is optional and bounded. Event can use an AI service to help review preparation evidence, formulate coach suggestions or assemble meal-planning drafts. Event applies deterministic checks and requires the athlete to accept changes; AI suggestions do not silently alter a training plan. The app uses Vercel's AI Gateway for its current optional coach features, which can route a request to the model provider selected for that feature.

Garmin-sourced data will not be sent to an AI system by default. Before doing so, we would need to explain the exact data involved, recipient/model provider, purposes and any international transfers; verify Garmin's applicable terms; and obtain separate, recorded explicit consent with an easy withdrawal mechanism. We do not intend to use Garmin-sourced personal data to train general-purpose AI models.

You can contact tom@good-ship.co.uk to ask about AI-related processing or withdraw a relevant consent. If the feature has not been enabled, no Garmin-derived AI processing has begun.

Service providers, storage and transfers

Event's application is deployed on Vercel and its saved workspaces use a hosted Postgres database with account authentication. Strava and, when available, Garmin operate their own connected services; optional AI processing may involve Vercel's AI Gateway and an underlying model provider. We use provider access only for the requested feature and do not sell activity or health information.

Some suppliers or sub-processors may process data outside the UK. The relevant cross-border safeguards and provider disclosures must be checked before Garmin Health data or AI prompts are enabled in production. Data about health and recovery will not be included in advertising audiences or sold to data brokers.

How long information is kept; disconnection and deletion

Saved event workspaces and activity-derived evidence are retained while an account remains active and the information is required to support training and the related features. Provider access tokens are retained only for as long as the connection is enabled or needed for orderly revocation. Operational and legal records may be retained for a limited additional period where necessary. We will publish and implement a concrete deletion schedule before wider public launch.

To disconnect Strava or future Garmin access, use the relevant connection controls when available. You can also ask us to delete stored athlete information by emailing tom@good-ship.co.uk. Provider disconnection and deletion of already imported records are different actions; we will explain what remains and why. Records Garmin or Strava retain in their own accounts are controlled by those providers.

Your rights and decisions

Depending on the basis and circumstances, you can ask for a copy of your data, corrections, erasure, restriction or portability. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw a consent you have given. Event's proposed training adaptation presents reviewable recommendations rather than taking solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. For concerns, contact us first or complain to the ICO.

Fitness guidance, not medical care

Event is designed to help people understand training evidence and prepare for races. Its outputs are not a diagnosis or substitute for individual medical advice. Devices and training-data providers may report incomplete or inaccurate information; Event aims to show uncertainty rather than turn missing data into false certainty.

Updates

We will update this page when Garmin integration becomes available, new data categories are added, provider arrangements change or the consent/deletion features are introduced. This public notice is not a substitute for implementing those controls in the app.
